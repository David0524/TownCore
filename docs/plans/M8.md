## Done when (CLAUDE.md §13)

Health dashboard, spend caps, adapter-failure alerts, evals in CI.

## Where we start

- **Spend caps:** a per-town daily AI spend cap and call cap (town config, default $5 and 400 calls), enforced in `callModel`, with every call logged to `ai_usage`. A launch has its own $25 cap (D-M7-11).
- **Alerts:** Mail-6 keeps one open alert per condition in `mail_alerts`, resolves it when it clears, and emails active admins at towncore.net (new, resolved, then daily while open). It covers mail conditions only.
- **Source health:** every ingest updates `sources.health` (ok, degraded, broken after 3 failures) and `source_runs`. Nobody is told when a source breaks.
- **Evals:** CI replays the recorded model responses (`pnpm evals`). Replays are deterministic: any failure fails CI, and a changed prompt fails until it's re-recorded. Re-recording (`pnpm evals --live`) overwrites the baseline without comparing.
- **Dashboard:** Admin has review, skip log, mail health, newsletter, directory and launches. There is no per-town operations view (§12 lists what it should show).

## Design

### 1. Health dashboard: Admin → Health

One row per town that isn't paused, over the last 7 days unless noted:

- items a day (published stories, guides excluded);
- fact-check pass rate (stories that passed on the first check);
- review queue: stories waiting and the oldest wait;
- corrections in the last 30 days;
- sources ok, degraded and broken, with the broken ones named;
- AI spend today against the cap, and AI cost per published story;
- active newsletter subscribers;
- paying businesses (active placements).

Open alerts (all kinds) are listed above the table.

### 2. Ops alerts, beside the mail ones

The same alert table and emails; the worker adds these conditions to the mail ones:

| Condition | Severity |
| --- | --- |
| A source is broken (3 failed runs in a row), for a draft or live town | critical for live towns, warning for drafts |
| A town reached its daily AI spend or call cap today | warning |
| AI cost per published story this week is more than 50% above last week (at least 5 stories each week) | warning (§8) |
| A story has waited in review for more than 24 hours | warning |
| The worker's heartbeat is more than 5 minutes old | critical |

Alert emails now say "TownCore alert" instead of naming mail.

### 3. Spend caps

The per-town caps stay as they are. New: a **network-wide daily AI cap** (config, default $50), checked in `callModel` beside the town's. Hitting it stops model calls everywhere for the day and opens a critical alert.

### 4. Evals in CI

- CI keeps replaying recordings.
- `pnpm evals --live` now compares the new run with the committed baseline (`last-report.json`) and **refuses to save a recording** that lowers the fact-check pass rate or raises the unsupported-claim count (or adds any safety failure), unless `--accept-regression` is passed with a reason that goes into the report.
- So a prompt change can't merge without a recording, and a recording can't silently make things worse.
