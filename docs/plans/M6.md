## Design

### 1. Seeding and categories

- **One category list for every town**, in config. It maps license types to reader-facing categories: General contractors, Roofers, Electricians, Plumbers, HVAC, Pool and spa, Restaurants, Bars and cafés, Real estate (after the real estate layout), and so on.
- **Sources:**
  - **Licensed businesses:** a seeding job turns DBPR license records into `businesses` rows, with `license_verified`, deduplicated by license number. Only active licenses are listed.
  - **New filings:** Sunbiz new corporations are **not listed automatically**. Many are holding companies or home addresses. They feed "New in town" and claim invitations instead (D-M6-1).
- **Privacy:** many contractor licenses belong to individuals at home addresses. Unclaimed listings show only the city and ZIP, never a street address, and no owner names beyond the licensed business name (D-M6-2).
- **Town limits:** a "{Town} mailing address" can be outside city limits (the 2026-10-15 decision). Listings say "{Town} area". Census geocoding (D-M4-1) marks each address in or out of city limits, for maps and a later filter.

### 2. Pages

- **Directory home:** categories with counts, and search.
- **Category page:**
  - `directory.category.exclusive` in the header;
  - `directory.category.top` (at most 3 sponsored listings, labeled), with `directory.topspot` as the first of them;
  - then organic listings, sorted by verified license, completeness and recency. Payment never affects organic order (Rule 6).
  - `directory.quote-request`: one form that goes to up to 3 sponsored businesses.
- **Business page:**
  - hours, contact and the "License verified" badge;
  - "In the news": our articles that mention the business, from M4's entities when they land, by name match until then;
  - a claim button if unclaimed;
  - the `directory.business.cta` slot (call button and "Get a quote");
  - LocalBusiness structured data.
- **Articles:** `article.related-businesses` shows businesses matched to the story's beat (permits and development → contractors; restaurant openings → restaurants), sponsored first and labeled.
- **Homepage modules:** `directory-featured`, `find-a-pro`, `new-now-open`. Each hides when empty.
- Directory pages are `noindex` until a category has at least 5 listings (thin-page rule, D-M4-5).

### 3. Claim flow and owner accounts

1. The owner presses "Claim this business" and enters their name, role and business email.
2. A one-time link goes to that address (D-M6-4: requested by the person, rate-limited, like double opt-in).
3. **The first claim of each listing waits for staff approval** in Admin → Directory → Claims, so nobody hijacks a competitor's listing.
4. Owners then sign in with an email link (no passwords) and edit hours, phone, website, description and service area. Photos come with M9 licensing.
5. Edits to the name or the license are reviewed by staff.

### 4. Stripe

- **Products per town, priced from the town config:**
  - **Enhanced listing:** $39/month. More fields shown, a highlighted card, the business can reply to reviews later.
  - **Sponsored slot:** $199/month. One of at most 3 per category, labeled.
  - **Category exclusive:** price to be set.
  - **Newsletter sponsorship:** built, off.
  - A CLI (`pnpm directory:stripe sync`, dry run by default) creates the Stripe Products and Prices from config.
- **Buying:** an owner who has claimed the listing buys from their portal through Stripe Checkout (subscription mode). Inventory is checked before Checkout and again in the webhook, so a fourth slot is refunded and never shown.
- **Webhooks** (`/api/stripe/webhook`, signature verified, stored once per event id like `mail_events`):
  - a completed checkout creates the `placements` row;
  - subscription updates and deletions keep `starts_at`, `ends_at` and `status` in step;
  - a failed invoice pauses the placement after Stripe's retries end.
- **Creatives:** a sponsored placement's creative (headline, body, link) becomes a `campaigns` row for its slot only after staff approve the content (D-M6-7). The "Sponsored" label is always added by code.
- **Billing changes:** owners change cards or cancel in the Stripe Customer Portal. Refunds happen in the Stripe dashboard by staff.

### 5. Leads, stats and proof reports

- **Quote form:** stored in `leads` (kind `form`) with the business and slot. It is sent to each claimed, paying business's email (D-M6-9), rate-limited, and protected by a honeypot.
- **Calls:** tracked numbers need a paid telephony service (stop-and-ask, D-M6-8). Until then a tap on the call button is logged as a `call` lead, and the real number is shown.
- **`slot_stats_daily`:** impressions (the slot was in view) and clicks per slot and campaign, from the same cookieless beacon as article counters (D-M4-3).
- **Monthly proof report** per paying business: views, clicks, calls and quote requests. It is shown in the owner portal and emailed on the 1st (D-M6-10).

### 6. Outreach drafts (§11)

When a published story mentions a new opening or a listed business, the system drafts an email for the shared inbox: "You were in today's story; your listing is here; claim it". A person reviews it and presses Send (Rule 10). Nothing is sent automatically.

### 7. Ad standards in CI

The M1 checks (labels, reserved heights, CLS, mobile density at most 30%, nothing between a headline and its first paragraph) are extended to directory category and business pages, on mobile and desktop.

## Decisions for the owner

| # | Question | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D-M6-1 | Which businesses are listed automatically? | Active **state-licensed** businesses only (DBPR: contractors, electricians, restaurants, then real estate). Sunbiz filings feed "New in town" and claim invitations, not listings. |
| D-M6-2 | How much of an unclaimed listing is shown? | Business name, license, category, city and ZIP. **No street address** until claimed (many are sole proprietors at home). |
| D-M6-3 | Listings outside city limits but with a town mailing address? | List them as "{Town} area". Geocode to mark in or out of city limits for maps and a filter later. |
| D-M6-4 | Claim verification email | A standing Rule 10 exception like double opt-in: only to the address the person just entered, rate-limited. **The first claim per listing needs staff approval.** |
| D-M6-5 | Stripe live mode | Build and test in Stripe **test mode**. Live keys go in only when you say so, per environment. Customers pay only through Checkout, by their own action. Staff never charge a card, and refunds happen in the Stripe dashboard. |
| D-M6-6 | Products at launch | Enhanced listing ($39) and Sponsored slot ($199) on. Category exclusive and newsletter sponsorship built but **off** until you set prices. |
| D-M6-7 | Sponsor creative review | Staff approve each new creative before it shows. Labels are added by code. |
| D-M6-8 | Tracked phone numbers (Twilio or similar, **paid**) | Not in M6. Log taps on the call button as leads, and revisit when a paying business asks for call proof. |
| D-M6-9 | Delivering quote-form leads | Email them to claimed, **paying** businesses only, rate-limited (they asked for leads by buying). Unclaimed businesses never get automatic email. |
| D-M6-10 | Monthly proof report | Show it in the owner portal, and email it on the 1st to paying businesses (account mail, like a receipt). |
| D-M6-11 | Owner sign-in | An email link, no passwords. One owner email per listing to start. |
