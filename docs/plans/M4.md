## Design

### 1. Discovery and SEO plumbing (§11 "SEO")

These routes are served per town by hostname, like every other page.

- **`/robots.txt`**
  - A live town allows crawling and points to its sitemaps.
  - A **draft or paused town disallows everything**, so test content never gets indexed.
- **`/sitemap.xml`** is a sitemap index that lists:
  - articles (by month)
  - beat pages
  - entity pages that are indexable (section 4)
  - guides
  - static pages
- **`/news-sitemap.xml`** lists Tier 1–3 published articles from the last 48 hours, at most 1,000. `news:name` is the site name.
- **`/feed.xml`** is the RSS feed for the whole site, and `/beat/<beat>/feed.xml` is one per beat.
- **Canonical URLs** on every page. Article pages also get accurate `datePublished` and `dateModified`; an update note or correction changes the modified date.
- **Structured data (JSON-LD):**
  - **Articles:** `NewsArticle` with headline, dates, image, `author` (the "{Site} Newsroom" organization) and `publisher` (the site as an organization, with its logo).
  - **About page:** `Organization`.
  - **Guides:** `Article`.
  - D-M4-4 covers how the site is named in this markup.
- **Test-only content:** articles with `test-` slugs, and anything on a non-live town, never appear in any sitemap or feed.

### 2. Ranking (§6a)

`rank = newsworthiness × freshness_decay(beat) × engagement_adjustment + pin_boost`

- **Freshness half-lives** go in config: alerts 6 h, public safety 12 h, civic and development 48 h, business 72 h, guides 5 days.
- **Engagement adjustment** is a smoothed click-through rate relative to the town average, capped at ±30%. The data comes from first-party, cookieless counters (D-M4-3). Until there's enough data it is 1.0.
- **Diversity rules:**
  - No more than 2 items in a row from the same beat.
  - Public safety is at most 20% of homepage slots.
- **Lead story:** the highest-ranked Tier 1–2 story, or a Tier 3 story only if a person approved it.
- **Sponsored content never changes the rank.** A test checks this.
- **Pins** (new `pins` table):
  - Staff can pin and unpin a story from `/admin`, with an expiry.
  - Severe or Extreme NWS warnings and road-closure stories pin themselves until they expire.
- **Where it applies:** the homepage modules, beat pages and the newsletter feed (M5) all use the same function, `rankArticles()` in `packages/pipeline/src/ranking.ts`.

### 3. Images (§11c): the router, cards, data graphics and maps

- **New `images` table:** id, article_id, kind, source_url, license, attribution, ai_generated, alt, approved_by.
- **Router:** for each article it picks the first allowed option for the beat (defaults in config), records the choice, and **always falls back to the typographic card**.
- **Typographic card:** the headline, a beat icon, the town's motif and its theme colors, rendered with Next's built-in `ImageResponse`. No image model is involved.
- **Data graphics:** SVG charts and table cards built only from the story's own records. Examples: a vote tally, budget lines, roundup counts. They use theme tokens, and the alt text is written from the data.
- **Maps** (D-M4-1):
  - **Geocoding:** the Census Geocoder, cached per address.
  - **Base map:** streets from a downloaded OpenStreetMap extract for Florida (Geofabrik). We clip it per town, simplify it and store it as GeoJSON. It is refreshed monthly by a job, not fetched per request.
  - **Rendering:** SVG in the town's theme, credited "© OpenStreetMap contributors".
  - **Block level only:** a point is snapped to the block and labeled with the street and block (for example "1200 block of SW 12th Ave"), never a home address (§11c).
  - **Public safety:** maps are never more precise than block level.
- **Open Graph images:** one per article, made from the chosen visual plus the headline in the town's theme, at 1200×630.
- **Alt text** is always written from what the image actually shows.

### 4. Entities and entity pages (§6a)

- **Extraction:** when an article is published, one routine model call extracts its entities. Kinds: business, street, neighborhood, school, public official, project, agency.
  - Each entity name must appear verbatim in the article, checked in code.
  - Results go to the new `entities` and `article_entities` tables.
- **Matching:** names are normalized per town, so "Palmetto Park Road" and "Palmetto Park Rd." are one entity. When unsure, the code keeps two separate entities rather than merge wrong ones.
- **Pages:** `/topics/<slug>` lists an entity's articles, newest first.
  - Pages are **`noindex` until they have 3 or more articles**, so thin pages never reach Google (D-M4-5).
  - Officials' pages show only what our articles say: no photos, no biographies.
- **Display:** article pages show "Related coverage" from `article_links` (from M3) and from shared entities.

### 5. Evergreen guides (§6a, §10 step 6)

- **What a guide is:** a new kind of story, written only from official pages, never from other outlets (Rule 3).
  - Sources: the city site, the county, the state, utilities.
  - The topic list is in config. For Boca: trash and recycling pickup, hurricane preparedness, building permits, parking, beach parks, and so on. The full list is in D-M4-2.
- **Source pages:** a `page` adapter fetches each guide's source pages through the polite fetcher (robots.txt, rate limit), stores the extracted text as records, and notices when a page changes.
- **Writing:** the `guide v1` prompt. Guides get the same fact-check and code checks as stories. They are Tier 1, but **the first batch for each town goes to review** (D-M4-2).
- **Refresh:** every 90 days, or as soon as a source page changes, through the follow-up machinery from M3. "Last checked" and "Updated" dates are shown on the guide.
- **Where they appear:** `/guides` and `/guides/<slug>`, in the sitemap, and linked from relevant beat pages.

### 6. First-party engagement counters (D-M4-3)

- **What's counted:** article views, plus clicks from the homepage and beat lists (the list position is recorded too).
- **How:** a small `beacon` request writes daily counters to a new `article_stats_daily` table (article, date, views, list impressions, list clicks).
- **Privacy:** no cookies, no IP addresses stored, no third-party scripts.
- **Use:** ranking (section 2) and the admin dashboard (M8). The Privacy page (M5) will describe it.

### 7. Data model changes

- **New tables:**
  - `images`
  - `pins`
  - `entities`, `article_entities`
  - `article_stats_daily`
  - `geocodes` (an address cache)
  - `town_basemaps` (the simplified streets)
- **Articles:** `dateModified` comes from `updated_at`. Guides become a new candidate and article kind.

## Work order (PRs auto-merge on green)

1. **PR A: discovery.** robots.txt (draft towns disallow everything), sitemap index, news sitemap, RSS, canonical tags, JSON-LD. e2e tests check that draft towns are never listed and that the markup is valid.
2. **PR B: ranking.** `rankArticles()`, freshness config, diversity rules, pins table, admin pin and unpin, auto-pins, and cookieless counters. Homepage and beat pages switch over to it.
3. **PR C: images, cards and OG.** The `images` table, the router, typographic cards, data graphics and Open Graph images. Every article then has a visual.
4. **PR D: entities.** Extraction, the entity tables, `/topics` pages with the noindex threshold, and related coverage on articles.
5. **PR E: maps** (needs D-M4-1). Census geocoding, the OSM extract pipeline and themed block-level SVG maps.
6. **PR F: evergreen guides.** The `page` adapter, the guide prompt and kind, the Boca topic list with verified source URLs, and a live run that produces 30 guides on the draft site.
7. **Status:** update CLAUDE.md, plus a live check of the Boca draft site.
