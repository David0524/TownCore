## How the score works

score = 0.25 business value + 0.2 households × income + 0.2 source readiness + 0.15 coverage gap + 0.1 cluster fit + 0.1 growth (weights in `packages/config/src/ranking.ts`). Each input is 0–1, ranked within this list.

- **Business value:** establishments in high-value categories (Home services 2381, Home services 2382, Home services 2383, Home services 5617, Legal 5411, Medical 6211, Dental 6212, Real estate agents and brokers 5312, Restaurants 7225), weighted (home services 1, legal, medical, dental and real estate 0.8, restaurants 0.6). Census publishes these counts by ZIP, not by town, so each ZIP's counts are shared out by the part of its land inside the town: an estimate.
- **Households × income:** ACS 2020–2024 households times median household income.
- **Growth:** Vintage 2025 population estimate against the 2020 base.
- **Cluster fit:** 1 next to a town we run, falling to 0 at 100 miles.
- **Source readiness and coverage gap are neutral (0.5) for every town for now,** so they don't change the order: source readiness until the launcher's light check runs on a shortlist (M7 PR D), news coverage until a Medill data license (D-M7-3). Rule 7 still applies at launch: a town that can't reach its daily items never goes live.
- **Search demand** is skipped (no permitted data source, §10a).

Source: U.S. Census Bureau: 2026 Gazetteer, Vintage 2025 population estimates, 2020–2024 American Community Survey 5-year estimates, 2023 ZIP Code Business Patterns and the 2020 ZCTA-to-place relationship file.
