# Draft: data license request to FHSAA

**For the owner to send.** Rule 10: Claude doesn't email outside organizations.

- **To:** Kenya Tolbert, Administrator, Communications Services, Florida High School Athletic Association. FHSAA's staff directory lists her for media rights agreements: ktolbert@fhsaa.org, 352-681-3761 ext. 330. Confirm on https://fhsaa.com/staff.aspx before sending; the listing came from a cached copy.
- **From:** the owner, from info@towncore.net or a towncore.net address.
- **Subject:** Request for a license to publish FHSAA schedules and scores

---

Dear Ms. Tolbert,

TownCore publishes local news sites for Florida towns, beginning with Boca Town Square
(bocatownsquare.com) in Boca Raton. Each site carries a Sports page for the town's high school
teams. We would like to license FHSAA's game schedules and final scores for member schools so our
readers can follow their local teams.

Your website's legal notice asks that its information not be included on other servers or
re-used without permission, so we haven't used it. We're asking for that permission now.

What we would publish:

- Team-level schedules and final scores only: date, sport, level, the two schools, venue and
  score. No player names, statistics, photos or other student information.
- Only games involving schools in the towns we cover, on those towns' sites.
- Attribution on every listing ("Schedules and scores: FHSAA") with a link back to fhsaa.com.
- No resale or redistribution of the data.

How we would get it is up to you: a data feed or export you provide, or automated access to
pages you name, at a rate you set. Our crawler identifies itself as `TownCoreBot/1.0
(info@towncore.net)` and follows robots.txt, including your Crawl-delay and Visit-time
settings.

We're glad to discuss terms, including any license fee, and to sign your standard media
agreement if one fits.

Thank you for considering it.
