## Found in the published roundups: mailing city is not city limits

The new-business roundup includes ZIPs 33428, 33496 and 33498: "Boca Raton" mailing addresses
that lie wholly or mostly outside the city. State records only give the mailing city, and
several ZIPs straddle the boundary, so a ZIP list wouldn't be exact either. Fixed by wording:
the roundups now say "with a Boca Raton mailing address" and note that such an address can be
outside the city limits. Exact city limits come with parcel lookups in M6.
